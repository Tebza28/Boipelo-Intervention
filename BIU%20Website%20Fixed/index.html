WHO WE ARE

A Potchefstroom unit built for fast, decisive intervention.

Boipelo Intervention Unit is a law enforcement and private security company operating out of Ikageng Road Industrial, Potchefstroom. We work with households, businesses and industrial sites across the North West, pairing armed reaction teams with technology that tells us what’s happening before we arrive.

Every operator is PSIRA registered and drilled on the same standard of conduct: identify the threat, contain it, and keep the people we protect informed the entire time.

  • Armed reaction teams stationed for rapid dispatch across Potchefstroom
  • 24-hour control room monitoring CCTV, alarms and radio traffic
  • K9 units, drone surveillance and armed guards under one command structure

What we cover

Six core services, one control room. Every unit below reports back to the same dispatch team.

Armed response

24/7 rapid intervention to alarms, panic triggers and incidents, dispatched from our Potchefstroom base.

CCTV & control room

Continuous monitoring and reporting from a manned control room, watching your property around the clock.

Guarding

Armed and unarmed guards stationed on-site, trained to deter, detect and hold a position under pressure.

Drone surveillance

Aerial coverage over large or hard-to-reach sites, feeding real-time intelligence back to our team on the ground.

K9 unit

Highly trained dogs and handlers for detection, deterrence and building searches.

VIP & executive protection

Discreet, professional close protection for individuals, families and business leaders.
